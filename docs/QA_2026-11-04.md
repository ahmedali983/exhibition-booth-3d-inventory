## Remaining limitations and validation gaps

- Individual FBX interoperability was not exhaustively tested against third-party exporter variants.
- Sketchfab's live 3D scene and thumbnail CDN require internet and remain hosted by Sketchfab.
- Protected, paid, and view-only meshes are neither mirrored nor downloaded.
- Imported files stay in the browser on one device; browser storage may be cleared or evicted.
- Procedurally generated DXF/SVG/BOM files are concepts only, not manufacturing-approved production files.
- In-site Sketchfab material recolouring is a transient preview. Some materials are texture-driven or may reject colour edits.
- Browser tests used representative examples, not all 5,209 catalogue cards or all 4,200 models as user-driven interaction sessions.
